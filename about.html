About Sanguine

Built so no request for blood goes unanswered.

Sanguine began as a front-end engineering project with one goal: shorten the distance between someone who can give blood and someone who urgently needs it.

Our mission

Blood cannot be manufactured — it can only come from another person, at the right time, in the right place. We built Sanguine to make that match happen faster: a single portal where donors, recipients, hospitals, and blood banks share real-time information.

Every feature, from availability toggles to emergency request feeds, exists to remove friction between "someone needs blood" and "someone gives it."

What we stand for

  • Speed — every second matters in an emergency request.
  • Transparency — clear eligibility and compatibility information.
  • Accessibility — a portal that works on any device, for anyone.
